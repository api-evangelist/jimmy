# Jimmy

> Jimmy (Jimmy Energy SAS) is a French nuclear-technology company that designs,
> builds and operates small modular high-temperature reactors ("thermal
> generators") that deliver carbon-free process heat directly to industrial
> sites. Jimmy is not a software company: it publishes no API, no developer
> portal, no SDK and no machine-readable specification of any kind. This file is
> generated by API Evangelist from the public company profile so that agents
> asking "does Jimmy have an API?" get an accurate answer instead of a guess.

## What Jimmy sells

- [Offer](https://www.jimmy-energy.eu/en/offer): Turnkey decarbonized industrial heat. Jimmy installs, operates and decommissions the generator; the customer buys heat under a secured 20-year supply-and-price contract.
- [Generator](https://www.jimmy-energy.eu/en/generator): A helium-cooled high-temperature reactor (HTR) micro-generator, described by the company as bus-sized, delivering process heat with no CO2 emissions at the point of use. Units are combined to match a site's thermal load.
- [Fuel](https://www.jimmy-energy.eu/en/fuel): The reactor fuel cycle and supply chain.
- [Industrial site](https://www.jimmy-energy.eu/en/industrial-site): Jimmy's own industrial platform at Le Creusot, Burgundy.
